## Privacy Policy for Archives Beyond

**Effective Date:** October 25, 2026

Archives Beyond ("the Extension") is a client-side browser extension designed to help users export their TTRPG data. This Privacy Policy explains our approach to data privacy and security.

### 1. No Data Collection or Processing

The developer of this Extension does not collect, store, track, or monitor any personal data, browsing history, or user credentials.
• All operations are performed entirely client-side within your browser instance.
• We do not operate external databases or backend servers to store your data.

### 2. API Keys and Storage

To use the Extension, you must provide your own API key generated through your account at myarchivist.ai.
• Your API key is stored locally on your device using the secure Chrome storage API.
• The developer has no access to your API key, your account configuration, or your data.

### 3. Third-Party Data Transmission

When you execute a synchronization or export, the Extension parses information from your active D&D Beyond tab and transmits it directly from your browser to the official Archivist AI servers (https://myarchivist.ai*).
• This connection is safe, direct, and authenticated using the API key you provide.
• No data is ever rerouted through, or shared with, the developer.
• Because data goes directly to Archivist AI, your data usage on their platform is governed by the Archivist AI Privacy Policy.
